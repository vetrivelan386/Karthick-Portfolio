Where I Stand Today

Early in the journey, and honest about it.

My first hands-on export was an onion shipment completed together with an established exporting team — from supplier negotiation and loading through to documentation and dispatch. That one shipment is the honest extent of my completed export history, and it is the reason I decided to do this full-time.

What I will not do is invent a ten-year track record. Instead I work with a process a buyer can verify at every step: you see the manufacturer, the samples, the test reports and the draft documents before anything ships.

Alongside export sourcing, I work day to day as the coordination point between buyers and manufacturing units over WhatsApp and calls — chasing production status, packing details and dispatch so buyers do not have to manage five contacts in five languages.

How I Work

Five steps, no surprises

01

Requirement in writing

You send product, grade, quantity, packing preference and destination port. I repeat it back in a written spec so nothing is assumed.

02

Supplier shortlist

I match your spec against verified manufacturers in my network and shortlist a couple of real options, not just one.

03

Sample & quotation

A sample is arranged where applicable, alongside a written quotation with ex-works, FOB and CIF breakdown.

04

Production & quality check

Once terms are agreed, production is tracked and goods are checked against your destination-market standard before packing.

05

Shipping & delivery

Invoice, packing list and certificates are shared for your approval before the container moves; the shipment is booked, tracked and delivered to your port.
